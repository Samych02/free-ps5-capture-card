# Free PS5 Capture Card 0.1.0

Capture your PS5's picture and game audio in OBS on your Mac—without an HDMI capture card. Play on your TV as usual; the app sends the Remote Play feed to OBS.

This is the **first developer preview**, using the tested build 8. Expect rough edges.

## Download

Download **`FreePS5CaptureCard-0.1.0-macos-arm64.zip`** below and unzip it.

You need an **Apple Silicon Mac with macOS 26 or later**, a PS5 and OBS on the same local network. Wired Ethernet is recommended. Intel Macs and Windows/Linux are not supported.

**The app is not notarized.** macOS may block it from opening. No changes to system security settings are required or recommended by this release; you can also build from source.

## Quick start

1. Enable **Settings → System → Remote Play → Enable Remote Play** on your PS5.
2. Open the app, sign in to PSN and pair your PS5.
3. Start with **H265, 1080p, 60 fps and 15 Mbps**, then click **Start Capture**.
4. In OBS, add a **Media Source**, turn **Local File** off, enter `tcp://127.0.0.1:9295` and set the input format to `matroska`.
5. Check for moving video and activity in the OBS audio mixer.

[Full setup guide](https://github.com/Samych02/free-ps5-capture-card/blob/main/docs/setup.md)

## Good to know

- The app captures video and game audio only—no preview, game controls or microphone audio.
- PS5 controller/microphone indicators still appear. An extra controller slot was seen in FIFA and is not confirmed fixed.
- HDR, precise audio/video sync, long sessions and launch on a clean Mac still need further testing. Independent code, security and license reviews are not complete.
- Only one OBS connection is supported. **Closing the window does not stop capture**—click Stop or quit the app.
- Protected content cannot be captured. HDR does not silently fall back to SDR.
- Pairing keys are saved in an **unencrypted, owner-only local file**, not Keychain. Other programs running as your Mac user may read it. Passwords, OAuth tokens and pairing codes are not saved.
- The local OBS feed has no authentication or encryption. Do not expose or forward its port.
