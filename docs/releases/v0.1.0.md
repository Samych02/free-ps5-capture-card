# Free PS5 Capture Card 0.1.0 — Developer preview

**Draft release. No app binary is attached while source-delivery and release-review
requirements remain open.**

Get your PS5's picture and game audio into OBS on your Mac without an HDMI capture
card. Play on your TV as usual; the app forwards compressed Remote Play video and
audio to one local OBS Media Source.

## What's included

- A native, single-window Mac app with browser sign-in and PS5 pairing.
- H264, H265 and H265 HDR options, with 720p/1080p and 30/60 fps settings.
- Video and game audio together in one Matroska stream for OBS.
- Capture status, recovery handling and optional troubleshooting metrics.
- No app-side preview, playback, controller input or microphone audio capture.

The accepted developer checkpoint is **0.1.0 build 8**. It retains the working
controller handshake and explicitly declares the remote microphone disconnected.
This does not remove the PS5's controller/microphone indicators.

## Requirements

- Apple Silicon Mac running macOS 26 or later.
- PS5 with Remote Play enabled, on the same local network as the Mac.
- OBS. Wired Ethernet is recommended.

Intel Macs, Windows, Linux and internet/WAN connections are not supported by this
build. Network conditions affect quality and latency.

## Known limitations

- The PS5 still shows controller/microphone indicators. An extra controller slot
  was observed in FIFA and is not confirmed resolved, despite no app controller
  input or microphone audio being sent.
- Basic operation has been reported working on a real PS5. Calibrated A/V sync,
  HDR output, long-session reliability and clean-machine launch are not fully
  validated; independent review is still outstanding.
- Only one OBS connection is supported. Closing the app window does not stop
  capture; use Stop or quit the app.
- Protected content cannot be captured. HDR never silently falls back to SDR.

## Privacy and security

Account identity and PS5 pairing keys are stored in an **unencrypted local file**
with owner-only permissions, not Keychain. Other processes running as your macOS
user can read it. Sony passwords, OAuth tokens and pairing/login codes are not
saved. Historical Keychain entries are left untouched.

The local OBS endpoint is **not authenticated or encrypted**. Other users and
programs on the Mac can connect to it. Do not expose or forward its port.

Developer bundles use ad-hoc signatures and are **not notarized**. No instruction
to disable Gatekeeper or change system security settings is provided.

## Before publishing an app download

- Assemble and verify complete matching source for the app, patched Chiaki,
  required submodules and applicable bundled non-system dependencies. Notices,
  recipes and GitHub's automatic repository archive alone are not sufficient.
- Complete independent license, code and security review.
- Complete the outstanding release validation, including clean-machine launch
  and the documented PS5/OBS acceptance checks.
- Freeze the binary/source inventory and confirm the signing/distribution plan.

See the repository's README for source-build instructions, and
`docs/source-distribution.md` and `docs/release-checklist.md` for the release gates.
